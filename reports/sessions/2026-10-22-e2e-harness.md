## Open risks

- Inngest `waitForEvent` may miss an event sent before the wait registers. The pipeline therefore re-reads the job row every 10 minutes at most (`src/inngest/analyze.ts`, `awaitWorker`). On a fast run, the worst-case added latency is up to 10 minutes per wait.
- A single signed PUT for uploads up to 4 GB: an interrupted upload restarts from zero.
